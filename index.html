Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy covers the eTalyp website (etalyp.datmddi.edu.tm) and the eTalyp mobile app (iOS and Android). eTalyp is the student information system of the Dowletmammet Azadi Turkmen National Institute of World Languages. The institute is responsible for the data.

Who uses it

eTalyp is used only by the institute's students, teachers and staff. The institute creates every account; nobody can sign up on the website or in the app.

What data is processed

  • Study records: surname, name, student number, faculty, specialty, group, year and study status; timetable, attendance, grades, lineup, make-ups, transcripts and certificates.
  • Personal file data (entered by the institute): date of birth, sex, nationality, address, phone numbers, document number, parents' names, workplaces, phones and email addresses, photo.
  • Account: username and password. The password is kept only as a one-way hash and is never stored in plain text. The chosen language and theme.
  • Technical data: the IP address and the browser or device name at sign-in and in the action log, for security.

The mobile app

  • The app connects only to the institute's server (etalyp.datmddi.edu.tm) over HTTPS.
  • Sign-in keys are kept in the phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore) and removed at sign-out. The password is not stored on the phone.
  • The app does not use location, contacts, the camera, the microphone, photos or files.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Why the data is used

  • To run teaching: timetables, attendance, grades, reports and documents.
  • To send the institute's messages to students, teachers and parents by SMS and email.
  • To protect the system: checking sign-ins, limiting wrong attempts and logging actions.

The data is not sold and is not shared with anyone for advertising.

Who sees the data

Everyone sees only what their role allows: a student sees only their own data, a teacher the groups they teach, a dean's office its own faculty, and the academic office and administrators all data. Data is given to third parties only where the law requires it.

Where and how long it is kept

The data is kept on the institute's own server in Turkmenistan and travels over HTTPS. Backups are encrypted and kept for a limited time.

Study records are kept while a person studies and afterwards under the archiving rules for study documents. A website session ends at sign-out or after a long idle time; the app's keys expire too.

Your rights

  • See your data on the website or in the app.
  • Have wrong data corrected through the dean's office or the academic office.
  • Ask for your account to be closed or your data deleted: write to the email below. Study documents are kept for the period the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and the date above is updated.
